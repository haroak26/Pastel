1. Toolbar horizontal padding

The toolbar currently has inconsistent internal padding.

Issue

The top and bottom padding around the buttons is correct.
However, the first and last buttons extend too close to the outer edge of the toolbar.
This makes the toolbar feel visually "stretched" horizontally.

Required fix

Increase the left and right internal padding of the toolbar.
The first button should sit the same visual distance from the left edge as the buttons sit from the top and bottom.
Likewise, the Export button should have the same spacing from the right edge.
The toolbar should feel perfectly centred inside its white rounded container.
Keep the button sizes exactly the same.
Do not shrink the buttons to solve this.
Do not change the spacing between toolbar controls unless absolutely necessary.
The only change should be the toolbar's horizontal content padding.

Think of the toolbar as having a single consistent padding value applied equally on all four sides.
