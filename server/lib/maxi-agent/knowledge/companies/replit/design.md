# Replit design language

## When to reach for this reference
Use Replit's design language when building developer tools with a consumer soul, AI-assisted coding products, learning-oriented builder apps, or any product that must make a technical activity feel inviting rather than intimidating. Reach for it when the brand needs warmth and momentum — warm paper-toned surfaces, confident orange actions, rounded editorial cards — for an audience of builders who want to start before they understand everything.

## Brand personality
Warmly ambitious and builder-first. Replit speaks like the encouraging senior engineer who hands you a running app and says "what will you build next?" The personality is optimistic, energetic, and unpretentious — it treats every visitor as a potential creator. It is playful enough to feel approachable, serious enough to be taken seriously as infrastructure: coral warmth for encouragement, navy depth for reliability.

## Color philosophy
Warm-paper with a hot orange action system. The canvas is a warm off-white (`#faf7f2`) with white paper surfaces (`#ffffff`) and warm hairline borders (`#e7dfd9`). Ink is a soft near-black (`#171723`) with warm gray muted (`#77747a`). The signature orange (`#ff4f18`) owns every primary action — buttons, links, the builder's spark — with coral (`#ffad98`) and red (`#ff6540`) as supporting warmth. Deep navy (`#282733`) is the grounding surface for contrast panels, footers, and statement sections. The system pairs warm neutrals with one hot accent and one deep counterweight; color feels like encouragement rather than branding noise.

## Typography approach
A rounded modern sans (Inter-class) with display-sized headlines and friendly weight. Hero statements run 42–52px at weight 400 with tight leading (0.98–1.0) — big, calm, and confident rather than shouted. Section headings are 26–34px; body is 13–15px at 1.35 leading; action labels are 11–13px medium. Headlines are often centered for announcement moments; working copy stays left-aligned. The type feels approachable because faces are round and weights are light-to-medium — nothing aggressive, everything encouraging.

## Spacing & density
Generous announcement rhythm with compact working panels. The content rail caps near 1180px with 32–56px desktop gutters and 20px mobile gutters. Section spacing runs 48–96px on marketing-style surfaces; cards and panels carry 16–24px padding with 12–16px internal gaps. Rounded panels of varying tint are the repeating unit — the page reads as a sequence of soft, distinct surfaces rather than one continuous canvas.

## Corner radius & shape language
Rounded and tactile — the defining element of the system. Radii run 6px (small controls) to 12px (cards) to 22px (large panels), with pills for buttons, chips, and the signature circular action. The geometry is the warm opposite of strict architectural tools: rounded rectangles that say "this is friendly territory." Panels may be irregular and tile-like in announcement moments, but working UI stays consistently rounded-rect.

## Elevation & depth
Soft, low, and selective. Cards and tiles rest on the paper background separated by tint and hairline borders; shadows are used sparingly (hovered tiles may lift 2px) and never applied indiscriminately. Deep surfaces (navy) create contrast sections that read as grounded anchors. The elevation language is "things sit on a warm desk" — light, tangible, and friendly, with no heavy architectural layering.

## Iconography & imagery
Simple, rounded, and illustrative. Icons are geometric with rounded joins at 16–24px; the brand's marks are built from basic shapes (crosses, sparks, geometric logos). Illustrations are minimal line art — small, friendly, and anchored to cards rather than dominating them. Imagery is warm and human: builders, workspaces, and product frames presented in soft, rounded containers. Nothing is photorealistic-heavy or corporate.

## Signature patterns
- **The builder prompt** — a single, large, rounded input that invites creation in plain language ("What will you build?")
- **The hot circular action** — one orange circular control that begins the build
- **Tinted tile mosaics** — irregular rounded panels in coral, gray, navy, and orange used for feature storytelling
- **Warm paper canvas** — off-white backgrounds that keep the whole system from feeling like a cold terminal
- **Scale reassurance** — "start small, scale fast" framing rendered as proof panels and testimonials
- **Contrast anchors** — deep navy surfaces used sparingly for weight and grounding

## Motion philosophy
Brisk, springy, and encouraging. Actions respond with quick transitions (100–200ms); buttons darken and press down 1px on click; tiles lift gently on hover; the prompt field expands as it focuses. Expansion and accordion animations run ~150ms with simple easing. The motion model is "the tool is alive and responsive," but it never performs — movement confirms the build is happening. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Voice & copy tone
Builder-first, short, and energizing. Copy issues direct invitations: "What will you build?", "Ship anything", "Start small. Scale fast." The tone assumes ambition and supplies confidence — it never lectures, never lists features where a promise will do. Labels are concrete and action-oriented; the voice is the encouraging mentor, not the enterprise vendor.

## Explicitly do not
- Do not reproduce Replit logos, wordmarks, the diamond mark, or trademarked assets
- Do not copy Replit's product copy, IDE/editor interface, or AI-agent UX verbatim
- Do not clone Replit's builder workflows or claim agent capabilities the product lacks
- This reference describes a design language to draw from — it is not a license to clone any Replit interface
