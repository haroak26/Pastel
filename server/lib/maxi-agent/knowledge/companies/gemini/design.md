# Gemini design language

## When to reach for this reference
Use Gemini's design language for AI assistants, search and answer products, and any tool that lives or dies by a single input. It fits calm, light-first utility interfaces aimed at general consumers who should not have to think about the interface at all. Also the reference for products where the input field is the entire product.

## Brand personality
Effortlessly helpful and almost weightless. Gemini's personality is that of a patient assistant: neutral, bright, and unopinionated, with a soft atmospheric glow that suggests capability without bragging. There is no noise, no illustration, no urgency — the product behaves like a quiet room where you ask a question. Friendliness is expressed through ease, not decoration.

## Color philosophy
A white-first, nearly colorless system: page `#FFFFFF`, ink `#303038`, muted text `#66666D`, and hairline separators `#E4E4E7`. The one atmospheric element is a soft pale blue glow `#D8EFFF` that hovers around the composer, functioning as a state cue rather than a decoration. Links and secondary text sit at `#414148`; there is no loud accent anywhere. Color exists only as ambient light around the point of interaction.

## Typography approach
A rounded, friendly sans (Google Sans-style) at restrained sizes: a 30px, weight-400, centered question heading, 16px navigation and composer text, and 13px legal and utility copy. Sizes are close together — the system never shouts through type. Calm and evenness are the priorities; emphasis comes from placement and whitespace.

## Spacing & density
Very low density on a 4px scale, with a 64px header and 24px padding giving the surface air. The composer zone is centered both horizontally and vertically, making whitespace the primary layout tool. Sections are sparse by design; emptiness is a feature that focuses attention on the single input.

## Corner radius & shape language
Rounded and pill-forward: 12px on standard surfaces, 28px on the composer, and full pills for the sign-in control and input ends. The pill shape is the system's signature — it makes the one important control read instantly as an input. Geometry is soft everywhere, with no sharp corners to be found.

## Elevation & depth
Deliberately flat with one luminous exception. The composer's soft blue glow creates a shallow pool of light around it — the only depth in the system — while everything else is white with hairline borders and a subtle shadow on the input pill itself. The glow reads as atmosphere, not elevation.

## Iconography & imagery
Icons are minimal, geometric, and confined to tiny utility controls pinned at the edges of the canvas — a plus, a microphone, a model chevron, a settings gear. There is no photography and no illustration; imagery would compete with the input's primacy. A small four-color sparkle is the only decorative gesture in the entire language.

## Signature patterns
- **Composer-first gravity** — one pill input dominates the canvas; everything else recedes to the edges
- **Atmospheric glow states** — soft blue light `#D8EFFF` surrounds the input as a state cue instead of illustration
- **Single control hierarchy** — plus, model selector, and microphone live inside one input as one family
- **Grow-not-scroll input** — the composer expands vertically as text wraps rather than scrolling
- **Soft blue focus** — focus is a gentle blue ring around the composer, never a harsh outline
- **Utility edges** — settings and account live as tiny icons at the corners, out of the way

## Motion philosophy
Motion is barely-there: hover underlines links, the sign-in pill darkens a shade, and the composer grows in height as content wraps. Transitions are soft, quick, and unobtrusive — consistent with the glow's gentleness. The product feels still until touched, and then it simply complies.

## Voice & copy tone
Neutral, helpful, and honest. Copy is plain instruction — "Ask Gemini" — and legal text plainly acknowledges the product's limitations. The tone never overpromises and never performs enthusiasm. It is the voice of a competent assistant that states facts and gets out of the way.

## Explicitly do not
- Do not reproduce Gemini logos, wordmarks, or trademarked assets
- Do not copy Gemini's UI copy or interaction patterns verbatim
- This reference describes a design language to draw from — it is not a license to clone any specific Gemini interface
