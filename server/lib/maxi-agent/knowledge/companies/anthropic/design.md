# Anthropic design language

## When to reach for this reference
Use Anthropic's design language for research-led, trust-sensitive brands: AI products, scientific and policy communications, and any organization that wants to be taken seriously without shouting. It fits warm, editorial, light-first interfaces for audiences — institutions, researchers, enterprise buyers — who respond to calm confidence and intellectual weight.

## Brand personality
Anthropic is the thoughtful one in the room. The brand pairs intellectual seriousness with quiet warmth: an editorial serif voice carried by an ivory-and-ink palette, with deep black panels that read as depth, not darkness. It never tries to be playful and never shouts — it states a position and lets the content stand. The personality is measured, assured, and slightly academic.

## Color philosophy
The system is warm off-white first: ivory `#F7F5EF` page with brighter paper `#FFFEFA` surfaces, near-black ink `#171714`, and muted text `#64635D`. Deep near-black `#151612` panels invert the hierarchy for gravity moments, with hairline rules `#D9D4C9` doing the separating. The only accent is a restrained terracotta `#DF8B66`, used sparingly as a wayfinding mark or focus ring — never as a background wash. Beige `#E7DECD` fills quieter cards. The discipline: warmth comes from the canvas, contrast from ink, and color stays a whisper.

## Typography approach
A dual-voice system: editorial serif display type for statements and thought-leadership headings (40–58px, weight 400), paired with a compact sans for corporate headlines (44–56px, weight 700) and all body text. Body sits at 14–16px, navigation at 11–13px, buttons at 12–14px. Serif moments are the brand's signature rhythm — they slow the reader down where the content matters most.

## Spacing & density
A strict 4px-based scale builds all rhythm. Page gutters run 42px on wide layouts, 32px on smaller panels, and 20px on mobile. Sections feel generous and unhurried — this is an editorial canvas with room to think, not a dense tool UI. Vertical padding is deliberate, and space itself signals importance.

## Corner radius & shape language
Gentle geometry with restraint: 6px on small controls, 12px on standard panels, 18px on the largest surfaces, and full pills for the few circular elements. Nothing is sharp-edged, nothing is aggressively rounded — shapes soften to feel approachable while staying formal. Radius is consistent across the system rather than used as decoration.

## Elevation & depth
Almost entirely flat. Depth is expressed through color contrast — ivory panels on paper, black panels inverting the page — rather than shadows. Drop shadows appear only as a subtle lift on the rare elevated card. Layers separate by tone and hairline rules, keeping the surface calm and legible.

## Iconography & imagery
Icons are minimal, geometric, and quietly drawn — line-based marks that never outcompete type. Sparse linear marks (branch-like, tree-like) occasionally echo the brand's structure, but the canvas stays clean. Photography and imagery are handled as tonal media inside panels, never as noisy decorative overlays; imagery supports the message instead of carrying it.

## Signature patterns
- **Inverted gravity moments** — deep black panels hold the page's weightiest statement, with ivory text and a single invert-on-hover control
- **Button inversion** — dark controls invert to ivory on hover; beige cards darken their border before anything else moves
- **Serif statement rhythm** — the display serif is reserved for the few lines the reader must pause on
- **Accordion disclosure** — detail rows expand with a vertical height transition and a plus that rotates to an x
- **2px offset focus rings** — focus is always a 2px terracotta ring with 2px offset
- **Color-and-border-first hover** — states change tone or border before any motion is applied

## Motion philosophy
Motion is minimal and functional. Hover is color or border change first, with pressed states moving controls at most 1–2px. The one signature movement is the accordion's vertical height expansion, which is quick and unanimated. Nothing bounces or floats; motion exists only to confirm state.

## Voice & copy tone
Short, concrete, and faithful — copy states facts without marketing claims. The tone assumes a skeptical, well-read audience: labels are precise, statements are understated, and the brand rarely congratulates the user. Serious subjects are treated with a straight, unembellished voice.
