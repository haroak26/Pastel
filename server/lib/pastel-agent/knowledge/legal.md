# Design Reference Legal Guidance

This is practical guidance, not legal advice. Have qualified counsel review any
commercial product or public distribution plan.

## Lower-risk use

- Use company screenshots privately as design references for the Pastel agent.
- Describe general design ideas such as layout, spacing, color relationships,
  typography hierarchy, and interaction patterns in `design.md` files.
- Build an original implementation using original or properly licensed assets.
- Keep reference screenshots access-controlled and limited to agent development
  and evaluation.

## Avoid

- Do not copy source code, proprietary illustrations, photography, logos, or
  distinctive branded artwork without permission or a suitable license.
- Do not redistribute reference screenshots as product content, marketing,
  training material, or a public asset library without confirming the rights.
- Do not make generated products appear sponsored by, affiliated with, or
  endorsed by the referenced company.
- Do not use a company's trademarks as the identity of a generated product.
- Do not bypass authentication, bot protection, paywalls, or other access
  controls when collecting references.

## Agent implementation rule

Treat screenshots as visual research, not assets to reproduce. Preserve broad
interaction and composition lessons while changing copy, imagery, logos, icons,
and other distinctive brand elements unless they are owned or licensed by us.
When a reference is blocked, incomplete, or contains unloaded content, document
that limitation instead of inferring or recreating the missing content.

The fact that another AI company uses similar references does not establish
that the use is lawful. Copyright, trademark, trade dress, website terms, and
jurisdiction-specific exceptions may all apply.
