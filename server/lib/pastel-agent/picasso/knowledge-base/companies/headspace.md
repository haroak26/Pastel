# Headspace design language

## When to reach for this reference
Use Headspace's design language when building wellness, meditation, mental health, sleep, or mindfulness products. It is the reference for interfaces that should feel like a deep exhale — calming, reassuring, and unhurried. Also ideal for habit-tracking apps, journaling tools, coaching platforms, or any product where the user's emotional state is fragile or elevated and the design must meet them with gentleness.

## Brand personality
Soft, nurturing, and quietly joyful. Headspace has the personality of a kind meditation teacher — patient, encouraging, and entirely non-judgmental. The brand radiates calm without drifting into sterility. There is whimsy in the character illustrations and warmth in the rounded shapes, but it never becomes saccharine or juvenile. It feels like a sanctuary: a place you go to feel better, not a product you use to be more productive.

## Color philosophy
A pastel-led, intentionally calming palette. The baseline is a warm off-white or very light cream background that feels like soft paper. The extended palette spans gentle pastel tones: soft coral (`#F4A261`), muted lavender (`#7B6FDE`), pale mint (`#6ECCAF`), buttery yellow (`#F6D365`), powdery blue (`#6BB8D6`), and warm blush pink. These colors appear in illustrations, category cards, and UI accents — never all at once, usually 2–3 per screen to maintain harmony. Gradients are soft and atmospheric: sky-like transitions from dawn pink to morning blue, or sunset orange to twilight purple. Saturation is moderated across the board — no color exceeds 50–60% saturation, and most are significantly lower. The dark mode shifts to deep, warm navies and plums with softened pastel accents that feel like evening rather than darkness.

## Typography approach
Gentle, round, and highly legible. Headspace uses a custom or heavily customized rounded sans-serif for all text — letterforms with generous x-heights, open counters, soft terminals, and a uniform stroke weight that never goes thin or delicate. Heading sizes are large but never imposing (24–36 px) with a bold or semibold weight. Body text is set at a comfortable 17–18 px with 1.6–1.7 line height — more generous than most apps, designed for contemplative reading on meditation and sleep content. Letter-spacing is slightly open (0.5–1% tracking) to improve legibility without feeling tracked-out. All-caps is absent except for very small timer labels. The type feels like a warm, well-lit paperback — easy on the eyes for extended reading in low-light conditions.

## Spacing & density
Open, airy, and deliberately slow. The layout uses plenty of breathing room: 20–32 px outer margins, 24–40 px spacing between content sections, and 16–24 px padding inside cards. The home screen is vertically paced to encourage scrolling as a calm, exploratory act rather than an efficient task. Buttons and touch targets are generous (48–56 px minimum). The meditation player screen is extremely minimal — a large, centered illustration or animation with sparse controls, reinforcing that the user's job is to be, not to do. Density is very low by any standard; emptiness is a feature, not a bug.

## Corner radius & shape language
Soft, organic, and thoroughly rounded. Every element receives generous rounding: cards use 16–24 px border-radius, buttons are pill-shaped or near-pill (20–28 px), and even the smallest UI components (tags, progress indicators, timer chips) get at least 8 px. Character illustrations are built from overlapping circles, blobs, and rounded shapes — no hard edges exist in the illustration library either. Progress bars and sliders have fully rounded caps. The shape language is deliberately anti-geometric and anti-rectilinear. It communicates safety, softness, and approachability at every visual touchpoint.

## Elevation & depth
Soft, atmospheric, and cloud-like. Cards float on diffused, tinted shadows (0–4 px blur, very low opacity with a warm or pastel hue) that feel like gentle lifts rather than hard layers. The meditation player background is often a full-screen gradient or illustrated scene with ambient depth created by layered organic shapes. Modals and sheets use a soft blurred backdrop — not a stark dimming overlay — that feels like a gentle fog rather than an obstruction. Drop shadows are always colored (never pure black) and matched to the content they elevate. The overall depth model says: "things lightly rest on the surface; nothing imposes."

## Iconography & imagery
Illustration-driven and organically shaped. The signature visual element is the cast of illustrated characters — simple, friendly, blob-like figures in soft pastel tones with minimal facial features and rounded body shapes. They appear in meditation scenes, empty states, loading screens, and achievement moments. Icons are filled rather than outlined, with 2+ px stroke weights, gentle curves, and rounded terminals at 24×24 px or larger. UI icons are consistent with the illustration style — soft, approachable, and deliberately imprecise in a way that feels hand-made. Photography is absent; the entire visual world is built in illustration, animation, and gentle color. This creates a cohesive, immersive calm that photography would disrupt.

## Signature patterns
- **The breathing exercise animation** — a circle or organic shape that expands and contracts at a guided pace, often synced with haptic feedback, creating a visceral calm
- **Day-by-day course progression** — a structured path visualized as a gentle sequence of illustrated cards, not unlike turning pages in a picture book
- **Session timer with radial progress** — a circular timer that fills with a soft pastel gradient as the meditation progresses, with a gentle chime at completion
- **Sleep environments** — ambient illustrated scenes with slow, subtle animation (falling leaves, drifting clouds, gentle rain) and layered audio soundscapes
- **Daily reset and check-in prompts** — a brief emotional check-in presented as a soft, non-judgmental question rather than a data-gathering form
- **Achievement celebrations** — illustrated characters and soft animated moments that celebrate streaks and milestones without gamified intensity
- **The "wake up" call notification** — a push notification that arrives gently, phrased as an invitation rather than a reminder

## Motion philosophy
Slow, fluid, and therapeutic. Transitions use 400–700 ms ease-in-out curves — deliberately slower than any other major product, calibrated to feel like a deep breath. The breathing exercise animation matches the guided pace exactly (4-7-8 or box breathing cadences). Page transitions fade softly rather than sliding. Illustrations animate with gentle, looping, ambient motion — drifting clouds, slowly blooming flowers, subtle character breathing. Loading states use slow pulsing or soft animated illustrations rather than spinners. The motion model says: "there is no rush; you are exactly where you need to be." All motion respects system-level reduce-motion settings and can be disabled entirely for users with vestibular sensitivities.

## Voice & copy tone
Warm, inviting, and gently instructional. Copy uses the second person ("you") to speak to the user directly but without pressure. Sentences are short and paced for calm. Meditation guidance is spoken-aloud friendly — rhythmic, reassuring, with natural pauses. UI copy is encouraging without being performative: "Take a moment," "Ready when you are," "Start your practice." Error messages are serene: "We're having a quiet moment. Try again in a bit." Push notifications are phrased as invitations, not demands: "A moment of calm is waiting." The voice never judges, never rushes, and never raises its metaphorical volume.

## Explicitly do not
- Do not reproduce Headspace logos, wordmarks, the orange dot symbol, or trademarked assets
- Do not copy Headspace's character illustrations, meditation scripts, or UI copy verbatim
- Do not build meditation or wellness apps that replicate Headspace's specific course structure, breathing exercises, or content library
- This reference describes a design language to draw from — it is not a license to clone any specific Headspace interface
