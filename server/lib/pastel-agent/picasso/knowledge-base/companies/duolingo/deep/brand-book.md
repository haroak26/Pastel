# Duolingo Brand Book — Deep Reference

## Brand Personality

**Playful. Energetic. Gamified.**

Duolingo is not an education platform — it's a game where you learn a language by accident. The brand takes the universal human love for games (progress, achievement, competition, rewards) and applies it to the traditionally tedious task of language learning. The result is a product that feels addictive in the same way a mobile game does.

### The Owl — Emotional Core

Duo the owl is the brand's most powerful asset. He is not a logo; he is a character with a personality:

- **Encouraging**: Cheers you on with energetic animations
- **Slightly passive-aggressive**: When you miss a day, he looks disappointed. The guilt is real.
- **Beloved**: Users have genuine emotional relationships with the owl
- **Expressive**: Has a full range of emotions — excited, sad, proud, encouraging, pleading
- **Meme-able**: The passive-aggressive streak made Duo a cultural icon far beyond the product

Duo's emotional expressions drive user behavior more than any notification or email ever could. The streak flame is so powerful that users maintain 1000+ day streaks just to avoid disappointing the owl.

### Emotional Spectrum

- **Before lesson**: Motivation, anticipation, "I can do this!"
- **During lesson**: Focus, challenge, flow state, slight pressure (lives system)
- **After lesson**: Achievement, pride, dopamine hit (XP, streak, sound effects)
- **Missed day**: Guilt, urgency (Duo's sad face, streak at risk)

### Brand Attributes (In Priority Order)

1. **Playful** — Never serious. Even mistakes are fun.
2. **Encouraging** — Positive reinforcement is the default. You CAN do this.
3. **Energetic** — High energy, fast pace, celebration-heavy
4. **Accessible** — Anyone can learn. No prerequisites. No judgment.
5. **Addictive** — The product is designed as a habit loop. Streaks, XP, leaderboards.

## Tone of Voice

### Core Principles

- **Friendly and conversational** — like a supportive friend, not a teacher
- **Encouraging and positive** — celebrate progress, de-emphasize mistakes
- **Humorous and playful** — jokes, puns, and Duo's personality shine through
- **Enthusiastic exclamation marks** — "You're on fire!" "Nice work!" "Wow!"
- **Short and punchy** — copy is snackable. Lessons are 3-5 minutes.

### Copy Patterns

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Achievement | Celebratory, energetic | "You're on fire! 🔥 5-day streak!" |
| Encouragement | Upbeat, supportive | "Keep it up! You're doing great!" |
| Lesson feedback | Positive, constructive | "Almost! The correct answer is..." |
| Error/retry | Light, non-judgmental | "Oops! Try one more time?" |
| Streak at risk | Urgent, Duo pleading | "Don't lose your 100-day streak!" |
| Push notification | Playful guilt | "These reminders don't write themselves..." |
| Empty states | Encouraging, inviting | "Ready to learn something new?" |
| Leaderboard taunt | Competitive, friendly | "You're just 10 XP behind Sarah!" |

### Words They Use

"streak" "XP" "level up" "achievement" "quest" "challenge" "compete"
"learn" "practice" "earn" "master" "crown" "chest" "gems" "booster"

### Words They Avoid

"study" "practice test" "exam" "grade" "fail" "curriculum" "lesson plan"
"educational" "academic" "proficiency" — anything that sounds like school

## Visual Identity

### The Green

Duolingo green (#58CC02) is the brand. Period. It's applied extensively and unapologetically:
- App icon: green background with white Duo
- Navigation bars: green
- Primary buttons: green
- Progress bars: green
- The entire product feels GREEN

This is the exact opposite of Airbnb's restrained accent strategy. Duolingo wants you to see green and instantly think "Duolingo" — it's brand color as market dominance.

### Gamification as Visual Language

Every screen communicates "this is a game":
- Progress bars everywhere (lessons, skills, daily goals, quests)
- XP counters prominently displayed
- Achievement badges with colorful, chunky designs
- Streak flame as sacred UI element
- Leadership boards with avatar + score
- Treasure chests, gems, and virtual currency
- Level-up animations with celebratory effects

### The Illustration Style

Duolingo characters and illustrations are:
- **Flat and bold** — thick outlines, solid colors, no gradients
- **Expressive** — exaggerated facial expressions, big eyes, clear emotions
- **Rounded** — everything is soft and friendly (pill shapes, round characters, circular avatars)
- **Colorful** — each character/skill has a distinct color
- **Animation-ready** — characters are designed with animation in mind (simple shapes, clear silhouettes)

### Signature Design Elements

1. **Duo the Owl** — on every screen, reacting to your progress
2. **Streak flame** — lives in the top bar, counts your daily practice streak
3. **XP counter** — top right, always visible, counts up with animations
4. **Progress bars** — thick (8-12px), colorful, animating forward
5. **Skill tree** — connected nodes showing the learning path
6. **Colorful skill icons** — each unit/skill has a distinctive color and icon
7. **Leaderboard** — friends ranked by XP, weekly competitions
8. **Hearts/lives** — life system borrowed directly from mobile game design

## Typography

Duolingo uses a custom rounded sans-serif — **Feijoa** (also referred to as Din Rounded in some sources). The typography is:

### Feijoa / Din Rounded Characteristics

- Rounded, friendly sans-serif
- Geometric construction with soft terminals
- High x-height for legibility at small sizes on mobile
- Multiple weights: Light, Regular, Medium, Bold, Black
- Optimized for mobile screens (clarity at small sizes)

### Typographic Hierarchy

| Element | Weight | Size | Notes |
|---------|--------|------|-------|
| Celebration text | Bold/Black | 32-48px | Large, impactful, often animated |
| Question text | Medium | 20-24px | Clear, easy to read quickly |
| Lesson prompts | Regular | 18-20px | Legible for target language text |
| Answer options | Medium | 16-18px | In pill-shaped buttons |
| Body/instruction | Regular | 14-16px | Clean, compact for mobile |
| Labels/counters | Medium | 12-14px | XP, streak, timer |
| Badge text | Bold | 10-12px | Achievements, indicators |

### Typography Principles

- **Bold for celebration** — you just earned XP! The type should feel like applause
- **Medium for interaction** — answer buttons, CTAs, anything you tap
- **Regular for instruction** — the "teacher voice" is clean and clear
- **Rounded = friendly** — the rounded terminals make even "You got it wrong" feel gentle
- **Large sizes for emotion** — when Duo is excited, the type is too

## Spacing Philosophy

Duolingo has two modes:
1. **Lesson mode**: Compact, game-like density. Information is tightly packed because the user is in a timed, focused flow state.
2. **Browse mode**: More generous spacing for skill tree, leaderboard, shop — areas where the user is exploring and deciding.

### Spacing Scale

| Context | Spacing |
|---------|---------|
| Lesson content area | 16-24px padding |
| Answer buttons | 8-12px apart (stack of pills) |
| Skill tree nodes | 24-32px between skills |
| Leaderboard entries | 12-16px between rows |
| Section headers | 24-32px top, 16px bottom |
| Celebration overlays | Full screen with centered content |
| Bottom sheets | 16px padding, compact |

### Density Logic

- **High density in lessons**: The user is answering questions rapidly. Low padding between answer options keeps the flow fast.
- **Medium density in browse**: Enough space to scan and decide but still mobile-optimized.
- **Low density in celebrations**: Full-screen, centered, generous — the payoff for the dense lesson experience.

## Color Philosophy

### The Green Doctrine

Duolingo green (#58CC02) is used everywhere. It's not an accent — it's the canvas.

- **App icon and brand mark**: Green background, white Duo
- **Navigation and system bars**: Green
- **Primary buttons**: Green with white text
- **Progress bars**: Green fill
- **Active/selected states**: Green
- **Links**: Green
- **The default expectation**: "Duolingo = this specific shade of green"

### Extended Palette

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Primary Green | `#58CC02` | Brand, navigation, CTAs, progress, active states |
| Dark Green | `#4AAE00` | Hover/pressed states, darker accents |
| Light Green | `#E5F6D3` | Highlights, selected backgrounds |
| Skill Blue | `#1CB0F6` | Language skill color 1 |
| Skill Purple | `#CE82FF` | Language skill color 2 |
| Skill Orange | `#FF9600` | Language skill color 3 |
| Skill Pink | `#FF4B4B` | Language skill color 4 |
| Skill Teal | `#2CE0C8` | Language skill color 5 |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Text on green, backgrounds, cards |
| Near Black | `#3C3C3C` | Primary text (warm-toned, not pure black) |
| Gray | `#777777` | Secondary text |
| Light Gray | `#E5E5E5` | Borders, dividers |
| XP Gold | `#FFD900` | XP icons, premium/gold elements |
| Heart Red | `#FF4B4B` | Lives/hearts, error states, danger |

### Color Application Rules

1. Green is the default. Start with green, use other colors only when needed for differentiation.
2. Each skill/unit in the tree gets a distinctive color — this helps navigation and recognition.
3. XP-related elements use gold (#FFD900) — it signals value and achievement.
4. Error/danger uses red (#FF4B4B) — visceral, gets attention immediately.
5. White is the secondary canvas — cards, sheets, and lesson content sit on white.

## Key Interaction Patterns

### Progress Bars
Duolingo uses progress bars obsessively. Every goal, skill, lesson, and quest has one:
- **Daily goal**: Ring-shaped progress around the flame icon
- **Skill progress**: Horizontal bar under each skill icon in the tree
- **Lesson progress**: Horizontal bar at the top of the lesson screen (tracks questions completed)
- **Quest progress**: Bar on the quest card showing completion
- **Crown/level progress**: Circular indicator on skill icons

### Streaks
The streak is the most sacred interaction in Duolingo:
- Displayed as a flame icon + number in the top bar
- Flame grows/animates on lesson completion
- Streak freeze available (purchased with gems) to protect streaks
- Notifications escalate: "Day 49!" → "Don't lose your 50-day streak!" → Duo with sad face
- Milestone celebrations at 7, 30, 50, 100, 365, 1000 day streaks

### XP & Rewards
XP is the dopamine delivery mechanism:
- Earned for every correct answer + bonus for completing lessons
- Displayed prominently during and after lessons
- Animates in (counts up, scales, bounces) on the lesson complete screen
- Doubled during "XP Boost" periods (encourages longer sessions)
- Weekly XP leaderboard creates competition

### Hearts/Lives
Borrowed directly from mobile games:
- 5 hearts maximum
- Lose a heart for each incorrect answer
- Run out of hearts = lesson ends (or refill with gems / practice)
- Visual: red heart icons, visibly deplete/restore
- Creates stakes and tension within the lesson

## What Makes Duolingo, Duolingo

- **Green EVERYWHERE** — the most aggressive brand color application in tech
- **Duo the Owl** — a character, not a logo; an emotional driver of the product
- **Gamification as the product** — it's not "gamified learning," it IS a game
- **Progress bars on everything** — visual feedback that learning is happening
- **Streak as sacred ritual** — daily habit formation visualized
- **Celebration as UX** — completing a lesson feels like winning a level
- **Mobile-native, app-first** — designed for 5-minute sessions on a phone
- **No friction between "play" and "learn"** — they are the same interaction

### Anti-Patterns (What Duolingo Is NOT)

- NOT a serious, academic learning tool (it's a game)
- NOT minimal or restrained (maximalist, colorful, loud)
- NOT dark mode as default (bright, saturated, energetic)
- NOT slow or contemplative (fast-paced, energetic)
- NOT text-heavy (visual, animated, interactive)
- NOT professional or corporate in tone (playful, casual)
- NOT designed for desktop primarily (mobile-first to the extreme)

## Brand Evolution Notes

- **2011**: Launched as a free, gamified language learning platform. Green owl from day one.
- **2013-2015**: Mobile apps launch. Gamification deepens (hearts, streaks introduced).
- **2017-2019**: Duo's personality develops — more expressive, more memes, more cultural presence. Crown levels introduced.
- **2020-2022**: Leaderboards, quests, and social features expand the competitive layer. Duo becomes a social media fixture.
- **2023+**: AI features (Duolingo Max), more language courses, deeper character integration with the "Duolingo Universe" of characters (Lily, Zari, Junior, etc.)
- **Key principle**: Every evolution makes it MORE game-like, not less. If it feels like school, it's wrong.
